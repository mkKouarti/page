Questions

What people ask before hiring us

Is the price really fixed?
Yes. The number on each service includes VAT and the whole job. If your case needs less, we take out what you won't use and the number drops. The final number goes in writing in the scope sheet.
How long does it take?
One to seven days once we have your material: text, logo and access. A simple site lands in two. A migration with mail and certificates runs to five or seven. The exact date goes in the scope sheet.
How do I pay?
Outside this website, by bank transfer or Bizum, once you approve the work. We issue an invoice with full tax details. This page takes no payments and stores no banking data.
What does each maintenance plan cover?
The number is flat per month per client, whether we maintain one server or five. Care applies patches, watches the service and fixes what breaks. Protected adds backups with tested restoration and alerts. Critical adds priority response and hours for small jobs.
Is the website really mine?
Yes. The domain registers in your name, the code gets handed to you and the hosting sits in your account. Tomorrow you can take everything without asking us.
I pay a monthly fee for a tool. Can you get rid of it?
Sometimes. We check whether an equivalent exists that you can host yourself. If it does, we set it up and charge three months of that fee. If it doesn't, we say so and charge nothing. We stay away from invoicing and anything with a legal obligation attached.
What do you need from me to start?
The text you want to show, your logo if you have one, and access to the domain if you already bought it. If you have none of that, we write a first draft and you correct it.
Where do you work?
Remotely, with clients in Spain and abroad. In Madrid we come in person when the job needs it: a server that doesn't reach the internet, an office where we need to see the setup.
I already have a website and I don't want to change it
Then look at the migration or the review. We can shift what you have to a cheaper server, or tell you where it sits exposed, without touching the design.
What do you do with my data?
We keep your name and your contact so we can reply, nothing else. We sell no data, run no advertising and set no tracking. The privacy policy spells it out.
